About

Radical

Radical draws every character from stroke data and colors its components by job: the component that carries the meaning, the component that hints at the sound, two ideas combined, a picture, or a component whose origin isn't recorded. Sound components are hints, rated by how close they still sound, since many pronunciations have drifted.

Text is read word by word with the CC-CEDICT dictionary, so pinyin follows the word. A dotted underline marks a lone character with more than one reading. Word lists are mainland-based, so a few Taiwan readings (訊息 xùnxí) may show the mainland form.

Credits: stroke and component data from Make Me a Hanzi (Arphic Public License and LGPL); word list from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0); Traditional/Simplified conversion by OpenCC; writing practice by Hanzi Writer (MIT). Sound uses your phone's own voice. Translations, word cards and photo reading use an AI model. Full credits and licenses are in the project's CREDITS.md.

Sound and motion

Sounds (gongs, guzheng, dizi, erhu)
Light on the characters
Light follows the phone's tilt

Sounds play at the moments that count: checking an answer, adding a word, finishing a character in writing practice, finishing a review, and when an easier page arrives. Picked at random from five per moment. The light gives characters a lacquer finish with soft sparks drifting over them; with tilt on, tipping the phone moves the light.
