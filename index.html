About

Radical draws every character from stroke data and colors its parts by job: the part that carries the meaning, the part that hints at the sound, two ideas combined, a picture, or the dictionary radical plus the rest. Sound parts are hints, rated by how close they still sound, since many pronunciations have drifted.

Text is read word by word with the CC-CEDICT dictionary, so pinyin follows the word. A dotted underline marks a lone character with more than one reading. Word lists are mainland-based, so a few Taiwan readings (訊息 xùnxí) may show the mainland form.

Credits: stroke and component data from Make Me a Hanzi (Arphic Public License and LGPL); word list from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0); Traditional/Simplified conversion by OpenCC; writing practice by Hanzi Writer (MIT).
