Radical

Static flashcards? Bogus. Get radical.

Every character is drawn stroke by stroke and colored by what each part does: meaning, sound, or picture. Read real Chinese, tap any word, and it becomes a card that brings it to life in real sentences.

Words, not characters. A character alone rarely means much. Radical counts the words you know, because words are what you read.
