Radical Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Upslope Labs LLC, Denver, Colorado

Radical is a Mandarin reading app. We collect only what the app needs to work. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Without an account

Your texts, saved words, flash card progress and settings stay on your device. If you use an AI feature while signed out (for example, explaining a sentence), the text you asked about is sent to our server and our AI provider to produce the answer. To limit signed-out use, we keep a salted, one-way hash of your network address for that day. We never store the address itself.

With an account

  • Email address: to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your words and progress: saved words, card edits, review schedules, daily tallies and settings, so they sync across your devices. We keep one backup copy per day.
  • AI use: for each AI request, the kind of request, its size and its estimated cost, with your email. We use this to set fair limits and keep costs down.
  • Friends (only if you use them): your username, first name and last initial, friend code, and a progress summary (words by stage, days and cards this week, streak). Only people you approve as friends see it.
  • Shared decks: when you share a deck, a copy of its words and your display name is saved so others can open it from the link.

What we send to others

We use a few services to run Radical. Each gets only what it needs:

  • Vercel hosts the app and our server.
  • Supabase handles sign-in and stores account data.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes and feedback emails.
  • Anthropic, our AI provider, receives the text needed to answer an AI request (a word, a sentence, a photo of class notes you choose to import). See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Wikipedia: articles you open in Explore are loaded from Wikipedia directly by your device.

Messages you send us

Feedback and reports from the app are emailed to us with your sign-in email so we can reply, and a copy is kept on our server.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, Account, Delete account. This erases your account copy, backups, friends, shared progress, AI history and the sign-in itself. Words on your device stay until you remove the app. Decks you shared stay available to people who already have the link. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

Radical isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
