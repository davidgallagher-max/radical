# Radical brand

What's decided about how Radical looks and sounds. Where something isn't decided yet, this file says so.

## Name and mark

- **Name:** Radical. Written RADICAL in the header, in wide-spaced capitals.
- **Mark:** 部 (bù, "radical" or "section") in cinnabar red beside the name: 部 RADICAL.
- **Company:** Upslope Labs LLC (upslopelabs.com). The seller name on the app stores is Upslope Labs LLC.
- **Line:** "Radical loves characters."

## What it should feel like

Classy, intellectual, addictive and beautiful. It should feel like a fine printed Chinese page and a scholar's desk, not a game. Characters are the star: everything else steps back.

- No cartoons, mascots, confetti or streak-shaming.
- Rewards are quiet and earned: a drawn 完 at the end of a session, 賀 for milestones, a woodblock tick when you rate a card.
- Streaks allow rest days and are never sold.

## Characters

- Every character is drawn from stroke data and colored by component: **meaning**, **sound**, or **origin unclear** (gray).
- Big characters get a slow moving point light, like lacquer under a lamp. Reading text gets a still lacquer finish with no moving highlights.
- Meaning and Sound must differ in lightness as well as hue, so red-green colorblind readers can tell them apart. (No current palette passes this yet. Fixing it is part of the palette decision.)

## Type

- **Chinese:** Noto Serif TC (falls back to PingFang TC, then Microsoft JhengHei).
- **Interface:** Zen Kaku Gothic New.
- **Brand and headings:** Shippori Mincho B1, heavy weight, wide letter spacing.
- Base size is 112% of the browser default. Small labels never go below 15px.

## Color

**Not decided.** Scholar's Ink is the current default. David leans toward Lacquer & Gold. The candidates are on the "Radical Palettes" comparison page: Lacquer & Gold, Scholar's Ink, Dunhuang, Sancai, Celadon & Cinnabar.

- **Scholar's Ink:** night-blue ink, jade and celadon.
- **Lacquer & Gold:** black lacquer, cinnabar, gold leaf and jade. Gold is decided: "gold leaf", a gradient finish (pale highlight to antique ochre, with a bright top edge) on one main button per screen, and thin gold outlines with gold lettering on every other selected control. Never flat yellow fills, and never close to the Sound color.
- Every look has dark and light versions.
- Other looks: Observatory, Plain ink (drawn strokes, one color) and Original (the normal font, no color or light).

## Words

- **Tabs:** 讀 Read, 探 Explore, 練 Practice, 記 Track. Chinese character first, short English verb under it.
- **Voice:** plain, short and adult. Say what something does and skip the cheerleading.
- **Never used:**
  - The word "Claude" in the app (errors say "AI service").
  - Em dashes in app text.
- **Scores:** 對 半 錯 (right, half, wrong) in green, yellow and red.
